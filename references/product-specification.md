# Product Specification Document Workflow

## Trigger Conditions
- User asks for "产品需求书" / "产品规格" / "spec sheet" / "产品开发文档"
- User has decided on a product and needs a detailed specification for factory communication
- User wants to formalize product requirements for sampling/production

## Input Requirements
Gather from user:
1. **Product name** — Chinese and English
2. **Target market** — Amazon US (default)
3. **Target price range**
4. **Target customer profile**
5. **Key differentiation points** — what makes this product different
6. **Competitor references** — which products to benchmark against

If user does not provide all details, make reasonable assumptions based on category analysis and mark them for confirmation.

## Differentiation Methodology

To identify meaningful differentiation points:
1. **Read competitor reviews** — look for 1-3 star reviews mentioning specific pain points
2. **Compare specifications** — find areas where competitors are weak (thin materials, poor design)
3. **Address assembly pain** — "组装复杂" is a universal complaint; tool-free assembly is a strong selling point
4. **Improve materials** — thicker steel, better coatings, solid vs mesh
5. **Add features** — wheels with locks, multiple doors, removable trays
6. **Color options** — offer what competitors don't

Each differentiation point should have a clear "卖点话术" (selling copy) that translates the technical improvement into a customer benefit.

## Quality Testing Standards

Include relevant testing based on product type:
- **Metal products**: welding quality, coating thickness, salt spray test, load test
- **Plastic products**: material safety, drop test, UV resistance
- **Electronic products**: CE/FCC certification, battery safety, water resistance
- **Pet products**: non-toxic materials, pet safety standards

Always reference US-relevant safety standards (ASTM, CPSC, etc.).
